What should I share before arriving?+

Send us your travel dates, guest count, and expected arrival time by phone or WhatsApp. We’ll discuss room availability and arrangements directly.

What if my flight plans change?+

Mountain weather can affect Lukla flights. Keep in touch with your airline or guide, and let our hotel team know if your arrival or departure changes.

Can I arrange meals and a return stay?+

Ask about our breakfast, lunch, and dinner choices, and share your expected return date. We’ll confirm meals and room availability with you.
