Privacy & guest data.

Hotel Ganesh Himal Lukla uses information you share to respond to enquiries, manage your stay, receive guest feedback, and maintain the newsletter list.

WhatsApp enquiries & saved reservations

The stay planner prepares your dates, guest count, room preference, and notes in your browser. It opens WhatsApp with those details; the hotel receives your message when you press Send in WhatsApp. The planner does not submit a separate website reservation record.

Previously saved website requests and hotel-recorded reservations remain on the hotel server for authenticated hotel administrators to manage. These may include contact details, travel dates, room preferences, and special requests. The hotel can send updates by email when delivery is configured.

Feedback & newsletters

Feedback stays private unless you explicitly consent to public sharing and the hotel publishes it. Email addresses are never included in public reviews. Newsletter signups are stored so the hotel can maintain its subscriber list. Contact the hotel to request removal from the list.

Browser features & providers

The welcome intro uses browser session information for navigation preferences. Administrator sign-in uses an expiring session cookie. The website loads Google Fonts and an embedded Google Maps view, obtains weather from Open-Meteo, and links to WhatsApp and TripAdvisor. These providers handle information according to their own policies when their services are used.

Contact about your details

To request correction or removal of personal details, or discuss a reservation change, contact our hotel team. Share your name and booked dates, or an existing request reference when relevant, so we can identify your arrangements.
