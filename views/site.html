    Useful answers before your stay

    A little clarity.
    An easier journey.

    Plan with confidence and speak directly with our hotel team about your arrangements.

    How do I reserve a room?

    Use the WhatsApp stay planner at the top of the Contact page, choose your dates and room preference, and press Send stay enquiry. WhatsApp opens with your details ready to send. You can also call our team. Your booking is confirmed directly with the hotel.

    How do I follow up on my enquiry?

    Reply in your WhatsApp conversation or call our team. Share your name and travel dates so we can discuss availability and confirm your arrangements.

    What do the displayed room prices include?

    Prices are the hotel's listed room rates. Please confirm the rate basis, meals, taxes, and any additional services with our team before agreeing to your booking.

    What if my flight or trekking plans change?

    Contact the hotel as soon as your plans change and share your name and booked dates. Mountain weather can affect flights, so also keep in touch with your airline or guide. The hotel will discuss revised dates and availability with you.

    Can I request meals, a group stay, or special arrangements?

    Yes. Choose the Group stay or Meals & arrival topic and include your guest count and special requests in the WhatsApp planner. Our team will confirm which arrangements can be provided.

    How do changes, cancellation, and payment work?

    Contact our hotel team in your WhatsApp conversation or by phone to request a change or cancellation. Payment arrangements, deposit requirements, and cancellation terms are confirmed directly with the hotel before you finalise your stay.
